About

Roberto Isaac Consulting AB is my independent consultancy. I'm a Platform Engineer and IT consultant with deep expertise in DevOps, Infrastructure as Code and platform building, backed by seven years of hands-on engineering across some of Sweden's most demanding technical environments.

My career spans platform engineering at Saab and a progression at Ericsson — from ICT engineer, through DevOps and network-tooling roles, up to Service Architect for the monitoring domain, where I designed service architecture and led it through security audits. That range means I can move fluently between low-level troubleshooting, automation, and high-level architecture and stakeholder work.

I thrive in small, engaged teams focused on automated, self-service platform services, and I'm driven by curiosity and a genuine interest in continuously learning new technology — including implementing agentic AI loops to accelerate development and raise code quality.
